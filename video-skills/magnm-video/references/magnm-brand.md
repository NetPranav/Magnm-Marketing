# Magnm brand (v2, 2026-10-17, from the CEO)
Logo: the CEO's real mark, metallic black/silver angular "M" (outputs/visuals/magnm-logo-official.png, cropped copy assets/logo.png). On light or lime grounds use mix-blend-mode:multiply so the white box vanishes. Never on a dark ground without a light plate. Do not use the old navy/cyan Canva logo.
Direction: minimal black and white as the base, colourful minimal as the accent.
Palette: stone #EFEFEC, ink #0A0A0A, one accent lime #C8F31D (CEO can swap), muted #6B6B66. No gradients, no glow, no shadows, no glass.
Type: Anton (heavy condensed, the key noun, huge), Instrument Serif Italic (connecting words), JetBrains Mono (labels, commands, captions), Geist (body). All OFL, via npm @fontsource.
Furniture: optional small mono labels and corner brackets as page details; NO persistent MAGNM label or logo on every card (logo once, near the end). Mix cuts with page folds, inversions, type masks and stillness (see research/reference-style.md).
Names: Magnm, Cero, LazyLayout. Voice respellings (TTS only): Magnum, Sero, Lazy Layout. Handles: @magnm.studios (Instagram), @Magnm_Studios (X). Site: magnm-org.vercel.app.

## Palette v3: rotation (CEO, 2026-10-18; replaces the single-lime rule)
Feedback: every film had the same stone/ink/lime palette, which got repetitive (an external Gemini review agreed). The references use colour per chapter (YOASOBI) and several light editorial fields (Edge).
- **Constant base:** ink #0A0A0A and stone #EFEFEC, for anchors, text and hard cuts.
- **Field set:** lime #C8F31D · sky #BCE3FF · mint #C7F5D9 · butter #FFE89E · coral #FF9F80 · lilac #DCCBFF.
- **Films:** pick 2-3 fields per film and give each chapter its own field (colour carries the structure). The finale or end card uses one of them, not always lime.
- **Cards:** one field per card. Over a week, rotate fields so the feed reads as a varied set.
- **No repeats:** check state/palette-log.md and never reuse the previous piece's main field. Log every piece.
- **Unchanged:** text on fields is always ink; flat fields only; no gradients, glow, shadows or glass; one field per frame (two only in a deliberate split-screen).
- **Fresh layouts:** before each film, an agy `visual-refs` task scouts 3 new kinetic or editorial references (Pinterest, motion portfolios, dev-tool launches). The film must use at least 2 interactions not used in the previous film (type mask over footage, page fold, split-screen beat sync, accent outline traces).
