# Dev-tool tones (replace brag's joke tones for Magnm work)

Brag's seven tones (references/upstream-brag/tones.md) remain available for playful posts. For Magnm's own marketing use these.

## builder (default)
Direct, builder-to-builder. First-person plural. Show the thing working in the first 3 seconds, then say what it is. No hype words. Calm pacing, 4-5 beats.
Hook examples: "Your terminal can't answer questions. Cero's can." / "From wireframe to animation, without writing the animation."

## demo-first
Almost no talking head: a real terminal or UI, one clear action, one result. Narration is two or three sentences total. For Cero command-and-output clips.

## build-in-public
Honest progress. What we tried, what broke, what shipped. Show work in progress, name the problem plainly. Good for LazyLayout.

## explainer
Problem, idea, how it works, who it's for. Diagrams and typography over footage. 45-90s.

## launch-teaser
Short, confident, cinematic restraint. One promise, one visual, date or waitlist call to action. Only use with real dates and real links.

## Voice (AI narration)
- Warm, clear, medium pace. Short sentences. Say numbers and commands naturally.
- Pronounce names consistently. Ask the user once how "Cero", "LazyLayout" and "Magnm" are said, record it in voice-and-captions.md, and if the TTS mispronounces a name, respell it phonetically in the narration script only (on-screen text always keeps the real spelling).
