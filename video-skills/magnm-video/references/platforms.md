# Platform formats (v1, tailor me; verify current limits before publishing)

| Platform | Format | Size | Length target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reel | vertical 9:16 | 1080x1920 | 20-45s (max longer allowed; shorter performs) | Keep key text out of the top ~250px and bottom ~350px (UI overlays). Captions burned in. |
| YouTube Short | vertical 9:16 | 1080x1920 | under 60s | Title + first-line description with keywords. |
| YouTube (standard) | landscape 16:9 | 1920x1080 | 60-180s walkthrough | Thumbnail separate (Designer). Chapters in description. |
| LinkedIn | square 1:1 or vertical 4:5 | 1080x1080 / 1080x1350 | 30-60s | Burned-in captions are essential; professional tone; lead with the problem solved. |
| X | landscape 16:9 or square | 1920x1080 / 1080x1080 | 15-45s | Tight hook; post native video, not a link. |

## Deriving cut-downs
1. Build the 9:16 master.
2. Re-layout (not just crop) to 1:1 and 16:9: move captions, scale UI.
3. One `final.mp4` per platform: `final_ig.mp4`, `final_yt.mp4`, `final_li.mp4`, `final_x.mp4`.

## Share copy per platform (write in share-copy.md)
- Instagram: caption (hook line, 2-3 short lines, call to action), 5-8 relevant hashtags, suggested cover frame.
- YouTube: title (under 70 chars), description (what, who, links), tags, chapters.
- LinkedIn: 3-6 line post, one question to invite comments, link in first comment.
- X: single tweet or short thread, video attached.
Never claim anything not shown or verified in the video.
