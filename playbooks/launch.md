# Launch playbook skeleton (v1, Growth Lead fills in per product)
T-6w: positioning locked, waitlist page live, repo/README ready, demo video recorded.
T-4w: build-in-public posts 2/week, collect first 20 interested users, start commenting in target communities (no promo).
T-2w: private beta to the interested users, gather feedback, fix top issues, prepare launch assets.
T-1w: schedule launch posts, line up early supporters (real users only), rehearse replies to likely objections.
T day: post on owned channels, then communities that allow it, answer every comment fast.
T+1 to T+2w: thank-you posts, publish lessons and numbers, collect testimonials only from real users with permission.
