# Growth strategies for Magnm / Cero (researched 2026-10-18; agy refreshes weekly)

Ordered by impact for a new open-source developer tool with no audience yet. Timings in IST.

## Now to 4 weeks (foundation)
1. **README as the landing page.** A 30-second demo GIF at the top, one-line pitch, install in one command, a "how it decides when to ask" section. It must be ready before any launch post. (Sources: dupple.com 2026 playbook.)
2. **Maintainer voice on X and LinkedIn.** Short build notes from the CEO's personal accounts outperform brand posts (dupple). The CMO drafts and the CEO approves each.
3. **Text + image cadence.** 2-4 card posts a week from `templates/card.py`, each showing one true behaviour (dialog, decline, Ctrl+C, read-only answer).
4. **Timely posts.** When news breaks about an AI agent running a destructive command, post a calm "this is why Cero shows the exact command" card within 24 hours. Facts only, no dunking on other products.
5. **Community labels.** Add `good first issue` and `help wanted` labels, answer every issue within 48 hours (dupple).
6. **Bounty pilot** (outputs/growth/20261018-bounty-plan.md) once the budget is approved.

## 4-8 weeks (launch moments)
7. **Show HN** once someone can download and try Cero without signing up. Show HN requires something people can try, and no upvote asking (news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html). Title: "Show HN: Cero – AI in your terminal that shows the exact command before it runs". Post Tue-Thu 19:30-22:30 IST (07:00-10:00 PT). Open with a comment covering the problem, what's different and the honest limitations, then reply to everything for 24-48 hours.
8. **Reddit:** post in subreddits that fit, following each one's self-promotion rules. Show, don't sell (dupple). Candidates: r/commandline, r/opensource, r/LocalLLaMA (built-in local model), r/macapps. agy checks the rules first.
9. **Awesome lists:** PRs to relevant curated lists (CLI apps, AI terminal tools, local LLM tools), one list per week.
10. **Tutorials:** dev.to and Hashnode posts like "How Cero decides when to ask before running a command", with real screenshots.

## Ongoing
11. **Integrations:** work with popular tools (Ollama is already supported per product facts), and write about each one.
12. **Conference CFPs and meetups:** local Indian dev meetups first.
13. **Product Hunt:** later, when there's a polished site and a small community to show up on the day.
14. **Analytics loop:** each week, see which proof posts drove profile visits or GitHub stars, and double down.

## Never
Bought engagement, fake accounts, upvote rings, mass DMs, misleading claims, competitor attacks. These get accounts banned and destroy trust.

Sources: https://dupple.com/learn/how-to-promote-your-open-source-project · https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html · https://docs.algora.io/bounties/workflow
