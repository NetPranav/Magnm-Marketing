# Crisis and backlash playbook (v1, tailor me)

## Severity
- L1 minor: one or two negative comments, a bug report, a skeptical question. Reply within a day.
- L2 notable: a critical post or thread gaining traction, repeated complaints, a public comparison that is unfair or fair. User approves the reply.
- L3 serious: a viral pile-on, security/privacy issue, plagiarism or licence accusation, legal threat, harassment, anything touching real people. Pause scheduled posts, user decides everything.

## Steps (all levels)
1. Listen first: Listener summarizes what is actually being said, with links. Separate fact, opinion, and rumour.
2. Check whether we are wrong. If yes, say so plainly, state the fix and when. If no, correct with evidence, once, politely.
3. Reply in the same place the criticism is, short, no defensiveness, no sarcasm, no jargon dodging.
4. Do not delete or hide criticism. Do not argue repeatedly. Do not use fake accounts to defend us.
5. Take heated threads to a private channel only if the person is open to it.
6. Pause promotion posts while an L2/L3 issue is active.
7. After it settles: write a short lessons note in outputs/community/ and update this playbook.

## Reply patterns
- Valid bug: "Thanks for reporting this. You're right, <what broke>. We're fixing it and will update here by <date>."
- Fair criticism: "Fair point. <what we agree with>. Here's what we're changing: <action>."
- Misunderstanding: "Thanks for asking. To clarify: <fact with source>. Happy to show it working: <link>."
- Unfair/bad-faith: one calm factual correction, then stop replying.
