# Skill: Editorial Advice

When advising on what to create next or picking weekly editorial slots:
- Review the brief, active content pillars, recent drafts via `list_content`, and current research notes via `search_research` and `read_repo_changes`.
- Propose 2 to 3 concrete content ideas instead of a vague topic list.
- For each idea, specify:
  - Target platform and content format (post, thread, blog, card, or video script).
  - Exact opening hook tailored to that platform.
  - Why now: cite concrete evidence (a recent repo commit, research note, or audience question).
  - Expected production effort (low, medium, high) and any factual or product-rule risk.
- End with a single top pick and a clear rationale explaining why it beats the alternatives on effort-versus-impact.
- Recommend slots on future dates when grounded in evidence; never invent urgency or unverified product hooks.
