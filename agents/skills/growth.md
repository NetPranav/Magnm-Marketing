# Skill: Growth & Distribution

When designing distribution plans, launch checklists, or growth experiments:
- Channel plan: match platforms and communities (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, GitHub, Discord, Hacker News, Product Hunt, developer newsletters) to the right product stage, weighing effort against expected payoff and citing each community's self-promotion rules.
- Launch plan: structure a dated checklist (from pre-launch preparation through launch day and follow-up) covering demo readiness, documentation, announcements, and feedback loops.
- Partnerships and outreach: identify relevant developer creators, newsletters, and communities, and draft plain, non-spam outreach messages for CEO review.
- Experiments: frame every experiment with a clear hypothesis, execution steps, estimated cost (if any), timeline, primary metric, and explicit success threshold.
- Hard constraints: never recommend spam, fake accounts, bought engagement, upvote rings, mass unsolicited DMs, or astroturfing. Any paid spend or bounty requires explicit CEO approval.
