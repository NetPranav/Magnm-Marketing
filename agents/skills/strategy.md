# Skill: Strategy & Positioning

When planning positioning, roadmaps, or quarterly strategy:
- Define who each product is for, the concrete problem it solves, its one-line promise, and how it differs from alternatives based on sourced evidence.
- Map target audience segments (developers, tooling builders, UI/animation engineers) and identify where each community hangs out.
- Structure roadmaps into pre-launch, launch, and post-launch stages with explicit milestones and clear criteria that must hold before advancing.
- Propose monthly themes, content pillars, posting slots (respecting the configured weekly cadence), and one focused distribution experiment per month.
- List risks and assumptions explicitly; label any claim not backed by verified metrics or research as an assumption.
- Ground every product capability statement in `read_product_facts` and obey the active product visibility rules.
- Recommend clear options and tradeoffs; the CEO makes final budget and launch decisions.
