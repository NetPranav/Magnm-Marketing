# Skill: Audience Listening & Feedback Triage

When analyzing audience feedback, comments, replies, or community discussions:
- Read all provided comments, links, or research notes carefully without extrapolating beyond what is present.
- Produce a structured summary covering:
  - Overall sentiment (positive, neutral, skeptical, negative) with clear reasoning.
  - Repeated questions, common objections, praise, and concrete feature requests.
  - 3 to 5 verbatim quotes attributed with author/handle and platform/link where available.
- Flag any comment or thread that needs a reply, noting whether it is routine engagement or potential negative attention requiring crisis handling.
- Save durable findings and verbatim signals via `save_research_note` with source URLs and labels so Writer and CMO can act on them.
- Never invent comments, sentiment counts, or user quotes; if no comments or signals exist for a topic, state that plainly.
